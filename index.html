PMI Are Always Ready to Help You & Your Family

Pakistan Medico International Social Welfare Association is purely a Pakistani Organization, founded on 18th May, 1971, by few like minded people of Pakistan. It was originally registered under Societies Act of XXI of 1860. On 30th April, 1979, it was then registered with the Directorate of Social Welfare, Government of Sind. It has been recognized by the Central Board of Revenue, Government of Pakistan, Under 47 Income Tax Act, as a charitable institution. It is known to various government agencies, such as Ministry of Health, Directorate of Social Welfare, Government of Sind, The Cabinet Division, Government of Pakistan and Administration of Karachi City apart from various relevant Departments of Central and Provincial Governments.

Pakistan Medical International

Social Welfare Association

Reg No. dsw / (S) 274 of 1979 Approved by CBR U / S 2 (36)(C)

AIMS AND OBJECTS

a) To help the citizens of Pakistan to uplift their social and economic standard. b) To find out ways and means to improve the health of poor and needy people of Pakistan. c) To create sense of social welfare responsibilities amongst the intelligentsia. d) To open or assist in opening charitable educational institutions such as Primary lower secondary/Technical schools and colleges for the poor people to provide education and wipe out illiteracy from the country. e) To open small projects like opening industrial homes, polytechnic institutions etc. for training people in different vocations and providing employment. f) To open charitable hospital for the poor people of the country. g) To help and assist government authorities or other recognized private institutions under 47-C.I.T. ACT by donating medicines, medical goods and equipments, cash or any other essential items. h) To run non-profit institutions like hospitals health centres and charge nominal fee to cover incidental expenses. i) To run and carry out all social, economic and medical relief work throughout Pakistan and other countries as well as during emergencies and national and natural catastrophies. j) To accept donations in cash and kind both within the country and outside the country after fulfilment of legal formalities. k) To raise and acquire loans to the extent of 10% of the annual budget of the society for the previous years.

ReHumaintarian Response to the 2005 Earthquake

Background of the Disaster • A powerful earthquake struck Northern Pakistan on October 8, 2005. • Magnitude: 7.6 10 • Thousands lost their lives; many more were injured or displaced. • Urgent need for medical and food support across devastated areas. PMI's Immediate Response • PMI mobilized doctors, paramedics, and volunteers within hours. • Set up emergency medical camps in the hardest-hit regions. • Provided first aid, trauma care, and emergency surgeries. • Established temporary shelters and relief centers

Flood Relief Efforts by PMI in Pakistan

Background of the Disaster • Torrential monsoon rains caused severe flooding across Pakistan. • Major impact in Sindh, Balochistan, and South Punjab. • Millions displaced; homes, crops, and infrastructure destroyed. • Urgent need for shelter, clean water, food, and medical assistance. PMI's Immediate Response • PMI mobilized thousands of volunteers, doctors, and emergency staff. • Established emergency response centers and medical camps. • Provided rescue operations in submerged regions. • Collaborated with local authorities and international organizations.
