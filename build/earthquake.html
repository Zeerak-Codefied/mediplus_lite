Humaintarian Response to the 2005 Earthquake

Humaintarian Response to the 2005 Earthquake

Date of mission: 8th October 2005

Location: Earthquake effected areas of Northern Pakistan

Objective: Immediate Relief through medical aid, food distribution and humanitarian assistance.

Background of the Disaster

• A powerful earthquake struck Northern Pakistan on October 8, 2005. • Magnitude: 7.6

• Thousands lost their lives; many more were injured or displaced.

• Urgent need for medical and food support across devastated areas. PMI's Immediate Response

• PMI mobilized doctors, paramedics, and volunteers within hours.

• Set up emergency medical camps in the hardest-hit regions.

• Provided first aid, trauma care, and emergency surgeries.

• Established temporary shelters and relief centers. Humanitarian Support

• Food supplies were distributed to thousands of affected families.

• Clean drinking water, warm clothes, and blankets were provided.

• Special attention was given to children, women, and the elderly.

• Psychological support was also offered to survivors. Collaboration and Dedication

• PMI worked in collaboration with local authorities and NGOs.

• All efforts were made with transparency, speed, and dedication.

• The mission showcased PMI's commitment to humanity and solidarity. Impact Summary

• Over 10,000 patients treated at medical camps.

• Thousands of food packets distributed.

• Critical lives saved through timely intervention.

• PMI's mission became a symbol of hope and compassion. Conclusion

• PMI remains committed to serving humanity in times of crisis.

• The 2005 mission stands as a proud example of rapid and effective disaster response.

• Together, we can continue to make a difference.
