Old Age Homes

Pakistan Medico International Old Age Homes

a) Old age homes, also known as senior care facilities, provide residential care and support services for elderly individuals. These services typically include shelter and accommodation, nutritious meals, medical care and health services, recreational and social activities, counseling and emotional support, and assistance with daily living. Some homes also offer more specialized services like physiotherapy, rehabilitation, and psychological support. Here's a more detailed look at the services offered:

Shelter and Accommodation:

• Old age homes provide safe and comfortable living spaces for residents.

• This may include individual rooms, dormitories, or communal areas.

Nutritious Meals:

• Residents are provided with balanced and nutritious meals, often prepared in-house.

• PMI homes also cater to dietary restrictions or preferences.

Medical Care and Health Services:

• Old age homes often have on-site medical staff, including nurses and doctors, to provide regular check-ups and medical care.

• They may also offer services like medication management, wound care, and assistance with mobility devices.

• PMI homes partner with local hospitals and clinics for specialized medical care.

Recreational and Social Activities:

• Old age homes offer a variety of recreational and social activities to promote engagement and well-being.

• These may include group outings, hobby clubs, arts and crafts, and religious services.

Counseling and Emotional Support:

• Residents are provided with access to mental health professionals who can offer counseling and emotional support.

• This may include individual therapy, group therapy, and support groups.

Assistance with Daily Living:

• Old age homes provide assistance with personal care, such as bathing, dressing, and toileting.

• They may also offer assistance with mobility, medication reminders, and other daily tasks.

Other Services:

• Some homes offer specialized services like physiotherapy, rehabilitation, and psychological health support.

• They may also provide transportation services, laundry services, and housekeeping services.
