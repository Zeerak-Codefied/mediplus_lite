PMI Aims & Objects

Pakistan Medico International Aims & Objects

a) To help the citizens of Pakistan to uplift their social and economic standard.

b) To find out ways and means to improve the health of poor and needy people of Pakistan.

c) To create sense of social welfare responsibilities amongst the intelligentsia.

d) To open or assist in opening charitable educational institutions such as Primary lower secondary/Technical schools and colleges for the poor people to provide education and wipe out illiteracy from the country.

e) To open small projects like opening industrial homes, polytechnic institutions etc. for training people in

different vocations and providing employment.

f) To open charitable hospital for the poor people of the country.

g) To help and assist government authorities or other recognized private institutions under 47-C.I.T. ACT by donating medicines, medical goods and equipments, cash or any other essential items.

h) To run non-profit institutions like hospitals health centres and charge nominal fee to cover incidental expenses.

i) To run and carry out all social, economic and medical relief work throughout Pakistan and other countries as well as during emergencies and national and natural catastrophies.

j) To accept donations in cash and kind both within the country and outside the country after fulfilment of legal formalities.

k) To raise and acquire loans to the extent of 10% of the annual budget of the society for the previous years.
